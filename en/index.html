What is a Support Network?

It is a system of people (families, friends, professionals, self-help groups) and resources that provide emotional, practical, and social support.

We help modify behavioral patterns and overcome dependencies by offering containment, understanding, and tools to build a new lifestyle, reducing isolation and vulnerability.
